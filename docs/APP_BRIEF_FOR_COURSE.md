# Brief: Integrating "Soundring Studio" (the App) into the Soundring Music School course materials

> **Who this is for:** the AI / writer producing and revising the Soundring Music School course scripts, lesson text, and video outlines.
> **Why it exists:** the existing course materials were written as a standard Skool course and contain **no mention of the companion app**. This brief gives you everything you need to weave the app in consistently — what it is, what it does, the exact user journey, the terminology to use, and drop-in CTA/script templates so every lesson matches.
> **One rule:** keep names and terms exactly as defined in the "Terminology" section. Consistency across 40+ lessons is the whole point.

---

## 1. The two halves of Soundring (read this first)

Soundring is **one experience delivered through two surfaces**:

1. **Soundring Music School** — the Skool community. This is *home*. It hosts the text and video courses, the discussion, the members, and the community groups. Everything students already know.
2. **Soundring Studio** — the companion web app *(working name — confirm before finalizing scripts)*. This is where students **bring the work they create and get it assessed by AI**. It is not a second course; it's the practice room / grading desk that sits beside the classroom.

**The mental model to teach students:** *"You learn it in the School. You do it and get scored in the Studio. Then you bring your result back to the community."* Every action, task, and challenge in the course is a doorway from the School into the Studio and back.

The Studio never replaces the School. It **completes the loop** the School starts: lesson → real work → AI feedback → reward → shared in the community.

---

## 2. What the Studio actually is (the elevator version for students)

> "Soundring Studio is your personal AI mentor for the *business* of your music career. Whenever a lesson asks you to create something — artwork, a bio, a post, a release plan, a track pitch — you upload it to the Studio and get an instant, honest score with specific feedback: what's strong, what's weak, exactly how to improve it, and your best next move. Do the work, get scored, earn your badge, and share the win with the community."

Keep that promise tight in scripts: **instant, specific, honest, actionable.** Not "an AI that chats" — an AI that *grades your real work against a professional rubric.*

## 3. Feature overview (what to reference in lessons)

Use these as the canonical feature list. Don't invent features not on this list.

| Feature | What it does | How to describe it in course copy |
|---|---|---|
| **Challenge upload** | Student uploads their artifact — image, audio, video, PDF, or a link — for a specific course challenge | "Upload your work to the Studio" |
| **AI Score (0–100)** | A rubric-based score for that challenge type | "Get your Soundring Score" |
| **Strengths** | What the student did well | "See what's working" |
| **Weaknesses** | Where it falls short | "See what's holding it back" |
| **Recommendations** | Specific, prioritized fixes | "Get your fixes" |
| **Highlights** | Standout moments worth celebrating | "Your highlights" |
| **Next Actions** | The single best next step | "Your next move" |
| **Points & Badges** | Rewards earned for completing/passing a challenge | "Earn your badge and points" |
| **Post to Community** | A one-click button that shares the result back into the School | "Share your win in the community" |

**Important accuracy note for scripts:** the "Post to Community" step is **one-click but student-initiated** — the student taps a button and confirms a pre-filled post. **Do not write copy that says results appear in the community "automatically" or "by themselves."** Say *"share it with one tap"* or *"post your result to the group."* This keeps expectations honest and the flow authentic (it reads as the student's own win, which is better anyway).

## 4. The canonical user journey (weave THIS into every challenge)

This is the exact loop to reinforce throughout the course. Every challenge lesson should follow it.

1. **Learn** — student watches/reads the lesson in the School.
2. **Create** — the lesson sets a real-world task ("Design your single artwork," "Write your artist bio," "Draft your 30-day release plan").
3. **Call to action** — the lesson ends with a button/link: **"Open Soundring Studio and submit your work."** It opens the Studio in a new tab, already scoped to that exact challenge.
4. **Upload** — student drops in their file or link.
5. **Get scored** — the AI returns a Soundring Score plus Strengths, Weaknesses, Recommendations, Highlights, and their Next Action — in seconds.
6. **Earn** — points and a badge are awarded for the challenge.
7. **Improve (optional loop)** — student can revise and resubmit to raise their score.
8. **Share** — one tap posts their result and badge into the relevant School community group for encouragement and accountability.
9. **Return** — student comes back to the School for the next lesson, momentum intact.

**Teach the loop early.** The onboarding lesson should walk the student through this whole cycle once, end-to-end, with a simple first challenge so they feel it before it matters.

## 5. First-time student setup (script this into the welcome/onboarding module)

Because the Studio is a separate app, the student links it to their School identity **once**:

1. From any challenge CTA, the student opens the Studio.
2. They sign in with their email (magic link — no password friction).
3. They confirm the email/handle they use in Soundring Music School so their shared posts land under their name.
4. Done — from then on, every CTA just opens straight to the challenge.

Scripts should frame this as a **30-second, one-time setup**, not a hurdle. Suggested line: *"First time you open the Studio, you'll link it to your Soundring account — takes 30 seconds, and you'll never do it again."*

## 6. Terminology (use these EXACT terms everywhere)

| Use this | Not this |
|---|---|
| **Soundring Music School** (the Skool) | "the platform", "the site", "our Skool" |
| **Soundring Studio** (the app) | "the app", "the tool", "the portal" *(pick the name and lock it)* |
| **Challenge** (a task that gets uploaded & scored) | "assignment", "homework", "task" *(pick one and stay consistent)* |
| **Soundring Score** | "grade", "rating", "mark" |
| **Recommendations / Next Action** | "advice", "tips" |
| **Badge / Points** | "reward", "trophy", "XP" *(unless you standardize on XP everywhere)* |
| **Share to the community / group** | "auto-post", "it posts automatically" |

Decide the ambiguous ones (Challenge vs. task; Points vs. XP; the app's final name) **before** the writer does a full pass, then apply globally.

## 7. Drop-in copy templates (reuse across lessons for consistency)

### 7a. Standard end-of-lesson CTA block
> **🎯 Your Challenge**
> [One sentence describing the specific thing to create.]
> When it's ready, **open Soundring Studio** and upload it. You'll get your Soundring Score and personalized feedback in seconds — then earn your badge and share it with the community.
> 👉 **[Open Soundring Studio →]**

### 7b. Onboarding explainer (welcome module only)
> Soundring is two things working together. **This School** is where you learn. **Soundring Studio** is where you put it into practice and get scored by AI. Every time a lesson gives you a Challenge, you'll create something real, upload it to the Studio, and get instant, honest feedback on exactly how to make it better — plus points, badges, and a win to share right here in the community. That loop — *learn, create, get scored, share* — is how Soundring turns lessons into an actual career.

### 7c. Micro-reminder (mid-lesson, optional)
> *(Remember: this is a Studio Challenge — you'll upload what you make for an AI score at the end.)*

### 7d. Post-challenge community nudge (for the group / pinned post)
> Just got your Soundring Score? **Tap "Share to the community"** and post it here. Celebrate the wins, ask for feedback on the weak spots, and cheer each other on. Consistency is the whole game.

## 8. Tone & framing rules

- **Position the AI as a mentor, not a judge.** Feedback is "how to get better," never "you failed."
- **Honest but encouraging.** The Score is real (that's the value), but every result ends on a Next Action so no one is left stuck.
- **The Studio is where the work becomes real.** Reinforce that watching lessons isn't the win — *doing the challenge and getting scored* is.
- **Community is the payoff, not an afterthought.** The share-back step is what turns solo work into accountability and momentum. Give it weight in scripts.
- **Never over-promise the AI.** It scores and advises on the *business/marketing craft* per a rubric. It does not judge musical talent, and it is not a human A&R. If relevant, say so plainly.

---

## 9. What each challenge lesson needs from the writer

For every lesson that contains a Challenge, the script/material should include:
1. A clear **creation task** (what to make).
2. The **CTA block** (template 7a) linking to the Studio.
3. What the student will be **scored on** in plain language (so they know the bar — this should mirror the Studio's rubric for that challenge type).
4. A reminder they can **revise and resubmit** to improve their Score.
5. A prompt to **share the result** in the community group.
